# Quantity Explosion of BOM (report)

The **Quantity Explosion of BOM** report shows a fully exploded, indented listing of all production BOM components for the item or items you specify, drilling down through every BOM level rather than stopping at the top level. For each component it calculates the quantity needed based on the parent item's quantities and unit of measure conversions, as of the calculation date you choose, so you can see exactly how much of each subcomponent is required to produce the parent item.

You can filter the report by item number, search description, and inventory posting group to limit the explosion to specific items or item groups, and by calculation date to control which BOM version and component validity dates (starting and ending dates) are used when the BOM is exploded.

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-99000753-scenario](../includes/report-99000753-scenario-include.md)]

Production planners can use the report to:

* Verify the full multi-level component structure of an item's production BOM before releasing a production order.
* Check calculated component quantities and unit of measure conversions for a specific future or past calculation date.
* Identify which BOM version and component lines are active based on starting and ending dates.,Manufacturing engineers can use the report to:

* Review nested production BOMs to confirm that sub-assemblies and their components are correctly structured across all levels.
* Validate that unit of measure conversions between parent items and components produce the expected total BOM quantity.
* Troubleshoot BOM setup issues, such as excessive nesting or circular references, using the indented level view.,Purchasing and inventory teams can use the report to:

* Estimate total component demand for an item by reviewing the exploded quantities across all BOM levels.
* Plan purchasing of raw materials and subcomponents based on the calculated BOM quantity for a target production date.
