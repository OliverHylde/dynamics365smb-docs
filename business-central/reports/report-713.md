# Inventory - Customer Sales (report)

The **Inventory - Customer Sales** report shows a list of customers who purchased selected items within a selected period, grouped by item and customer. For each item and customer combination, the report displays invoiced quantity, sales amount, discount amount, profit, and profit percentage, with subtotals per item and grand totals across all items. It's designed to help you understand sales trends, evaluate customer purchasing patterns, and assess the relationship between discounts, sales volume, and profitability.

You can filter the report by item number, item number 2, search description, assembly BOM, and inventory posting group to limit the report to specific items or item groups. You can also filter item ledger entries by posting date to restrict results to a specific period, and by source number to focus on specific customers. Additional filters on variant code, location code, and global dimension codes let you narrow results to specific inventory segments.

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-713-scenario](../includes/report-713-scenario-include.md)]

Sales and marketing teams can use the report to:

* Identify which customers are buying specific items during a selected period to spot purchasing trends.
* Compare invoiced quantity and sales amount across customers to prioritize outreach or account management.
* Review discount amounts given per customer and item to evaluate the effectiveness of pricing and promotions.,Controllers and finance teams can use the report to:

* Analyze profit and profit percentage by item and customer to identify low-margin sales relationships.
* Use item subtotals and report totals to reconcile sales and profit figures for a period.
* Assess the impact of discounts on overall profitability across the customer base.,Inventory managers can use the report to:

* Determine which items are most frequently purchased by which customers to optimize stocking decisions.
* Use the report alongside quantity and unit of measure data to plan replenishment based on customer demand patterns.
