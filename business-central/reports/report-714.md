# Inventory - Vendor Purchases (report)

The **Inventory - Vendor Purchases** report lists the vendors your company has purchased items from within a selected period, grouped by item. For each item and vendor combination it shows the invoiced quantity, the actual cost amount, and the discount amount, along with subtotals per item and grand totals for the whole report.

You can filter the report by item number, item number 2, search description, assembly BOM, and inventory posting group to limit the report to specific items, and by posting date, source number, and source posting group on the value entries to narrow the results to a specific period or vendor.

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-714-scenario](../includes/report-714-scenario-include.md)]

Inventory managers can use the report to:

* Review which vendors supplied a given item over a selected time period.
* Compare invoiced quantities across vendors to identify your primary suppliers for an item.
* Spot items with unusually high or low purchase volume from specific vendors to guide procurement decisions.,Controllers and finance teams can use the report to:

* Analyze actual cost amounts and discount amounts by vendor to evaluate purchasing terms.
* Verify that discount agreements with vendors are being applied consistently across item purchases.
* Use subtotals per item and overall totals to reconcile purchase costs during period-end reviews.,Purchasing agents can use the report to:

* Identify opportunities to consolidate purchases with vendors offering better discounts.
* Track vendor performance on cost and discount trends to support renegotiation of purchase agreements.
