# Payment Practice (report)

The **Payment Practice** report summarizes how your company pays vendors during a specified period, showing the average agreed and actual payment periods, the percentage of invoices paid on time, and related statistics such as median, mode, and percentile payment times. It also breaks results down by payment period, vendor company size, or source type, depending on the layout used, giving both an aggregate view and a detailed line-by-line breakdown.

You can filter the report by starting date and ending date to define the reporting period, by aggregation type to control how the payment statistics are summarized, and by header type to select which version of the payment practice data set to report on.

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-685-scenario](../includes/report-685-scenario-include.md)]

Controllers and finance teams can use the report to:

* Produce the statutory payment practice statistics required for regulatory filings covering a specific reporting period.
* Compare the average agreed payment period against the average actual payment period to spot payment delays.
* Track the percentage of invoices paid on time to monitor compliance with vendor payment terms.
* Review median, mode, and percentile payment times to understand the distribution of payment performance beyond simple averages.,Accounts payable managers can use the report to:

* Break down payment performance by company size code to see whether small businesses are paid differently than larger vendors.
* Analyze payment period lines to identify which time bands (e.g., 0-30 days, 31-60 days) account for the largest share of payments.
* Check the percentage of payments made through Peppol to gauge electronic invoicing adoption.
* Use the small business payment share to confirm fair treatment of smaller suppliers before submitting compliance reports.
